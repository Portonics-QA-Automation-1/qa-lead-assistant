# Meeting summary template

Title, date/time, attendees (QA team members marked), source note link.

## Update
Per team/stream, one line each. Facts only.

## Blockers (and where)
- Blocker: what is blocked | Where: system/team/person | Since: date | Owner

## Decisions
Only things actually decided, not discussed.

## Action items
| Owner | Action | Due (write "no date" if missing) |
Mark rows owned by the lead or a QA team member (config/team.json) with **[QA]**.

## Questions / follow-up
Open questions and who must answer.

## Risk flags (for the lead)
Escalation risk: blocker with no owner/date, QA item due today, dependency on another team, release impact.

Rules: never invent a due date or owner; quote ambiguous items as "unclear"; no secrets.
